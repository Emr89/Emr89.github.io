How NFL Performance Dimensions Fit Together

Applied multivariate statistics, with explicit assumptions and repaired mathematics.

The question

Do team performance measures contain shared structure that is easier to understand jointly? The original coursework applied covariance analysis, multivariate inference, dimension reduction and factor analysis to team-game statistics.

Mathematics matters

The reconstruction corrects a mean vector computed across games instead of variables and an incorrect Bonferroni critical value. The new interval helper checks mean-response intervals against standard linear-model calculations.

Interpretation

Components and factors describe covariance in this historical sample. They do not establish stable team identities or causal football mechanisms. Scores and contemporaneous performance can dominate the structure, and two teams in one game are dependent observations.

Evidence boundary

Base-R numerical tests pass. The original workbook is missing, so historical tables and football conclusions have not been regenerated. High-dimensional inference needs a separate rank and sample-size review.
