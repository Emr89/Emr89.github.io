Football Strategy and Decision Intelligence

Can historical team performance relationships support useful matchup questions?

The football question

I explored passing, rushing and opponent performance using regression, subset selection and random forests, including an existing Chargers prediction notebook. This work showed my interest in how performance dimensions connect. It remains historical research.

What the audit changed

Same-game yards, scores, completion rates and turnovers can explain completed games. They are unavailable for a genuine pregame forecast. Random row splits can also put both team perspectives from one game across training and evaluation.

Implemented repair

Malformed model-search arguments now parse in the repaired source. The remaining archive contains missing Excel inputs and five other files with syntax failures. No new predictive accuracy is claimed.

How this connects to Counterpunch

The new project studies play-level situational outcomes with a chronological test. The older game-level work supplies the historical motivation, without being relabeled as the new model.
