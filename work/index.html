How these will evolve

Old projects, rebuilt with better questions.

Several of these projects began as coursework, competitions, or early independent analysis. Rather than hiding that history, I am rebuilding them as modern case studies that preserve the original work while adding stronger validation, clearer storytelling, better visuals, and explicit discussion of limitations.

The code and original artifacts remain useful provenance. The portfolio layer will focus on reasoning, evidence, football meaning, and what I would improve today.
