The Lab

A place for projects that are too interesting to ignore even when they are not yet polished enough to belong in the main professional case-study collection.

Why it exists

Curiosity deserves somewhere to live.

Some ideas start as questions, prototypes, songs, visualizations, or experiments. The Lab lets those ideas exist without pretending they are finished products — and without cluttering the recruiter-facing core of the portfolio.

Music

AI-assisted songs can live here — with context.

Rather than dropping audio files onto a professional site with no explanation, each piece can be presented as a creative experiment: what inspired it, what role AI played, what you changed or directed, and why the result is meaningful to you.

Before directly hosting any Suno track, we should verify the plan and usage rights associated with that specific generation. Until then, this section can hold descriptions, artwork, and links without making unnecessary ownership claims.
