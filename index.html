NFL Big Data Bowl · Tracking Data

Finding Kick-Return Structure at the Moment of Reception

Using NFL player-tracking data, I studied how spacing, speed, positioning, and unit structure at the instant a kick is received relate to eventual return performance. The work grew into an NFL mentorship experience and shaped a question that still drives my analytics today: what can structure tell us before the outcome occurs?

Continental Football Alliance

Football as a system.

Football is usually presented as a sequence of outcomes: a completion, a sack, a touchdown, a win. Underneath every outcome is a moving system of spacing, timing, assignments, decisions, constraints, and interactions.

CFA is my attempt to make that deeper layer explorable through analytics, simulation, visualization, and interactive presentation.
