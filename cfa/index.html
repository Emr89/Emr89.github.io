Independent football R&D

Continental Football Alliance

An ongoing experiment in combining football analytics, simulation, tracking data, strategy representation, visualization, and broadcast design into one explorable system.

The question

What if football could be explored as a system, not just watched as a sequence of outcomes?

Most football analysis begins after the play: completion, pressure, explosive gain, touchdown. CFA begins one layer deeper — with the moving structure that produces those outcomes.

The project is designed around interactions: spacing, timing, assignments, player movement, strategy, game state, and how all of those elements can be represented in a way that is both analytically useful and visually understandable.

What CFA brings together

One project, several disciplines.

Football Analytics

Model the structure of the game.

Tracking concepts, strategy representation, matchup logic, situational context, and analytical interpretation.

Simulation & Visualization

Make the data spatial and explorable.

Represent player and ball movement in environments where plays can be inspected rather than reduced to rows in a table.

Broadcast & Product

Turn complexity into something people can follow.

Presentation, switching, teaching moments, interactive surfaces, and ways to surface meaningful football context at the right time.
