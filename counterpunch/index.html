Chargers Counterpunch

Situational evidence, opponent research and a film-review question.

Independent research prototype in private review. No Chargers or NFL affiliation or endorsement.

From situational evidence to a film-review question

Counterpunch investigates how observed offensive outcomes compare with a historical situational benchmark, then asks what an analyst should inspect against an opponent. The research separates model evaluation, sample uncertainty and football hypotheses.

What the prototype contains

A chronological evaluation workflow, a working evidence explorer, an opponent preparation example and explicit treatment of sparse samples. It does not infer optimal calls or assume access to proprietary pressure, personnel or tracking feeds.

Publication status

The source repository, empirical results, application and employer brief remain private pending review. This page reserves the portfolio location. Public results and an accessible demonstration will be added after that review.
